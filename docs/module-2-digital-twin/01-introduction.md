# Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

*Focus: Physics simulation and environment building.*

The future of AI extends beyond digital spaces into the physical world. This module introduces Physical AI—AI systems that function in reality and comprehend physical laws. You will learn to design, simulate, and deploy humanoid robots in simulated environments.

This module covers:
- **Simulating physics, gravity, and collisions in Gazebo**: Learn to create realistic physical interactions.
- **High-fidelity rendering and human-robot interaction in Unity**: Explore advanced visualization and interaction techniques.
- **Simulating sensors: LiDAR, Depth Cameras, and IMUs**: Understand how to simulate various robot sensors for data collection and perception.
